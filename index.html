Frequently Asked Questions

Why did you build this tool?

Most online services eventually become paid or limit your daily usage. We built videotomp3.tools to provide a simple, private tool that just works. No hidden fees, no limits, and 100% free forever.

Are my video files uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly. 0% server upload. Your file never leaves your device.

Do I need to sign up or create an account?

No! You can start converting immediately without any registration or email. Just drop your file and begin - it's that simple.

Which video formats are supported?

You can convert MP4, MOV, AVI, WEBM, MKV, M4V, MPEG, FLV, and WMV files to MP3 audio.

What is the maximum file size limit?

There is no artificial limit. Because conversion happens locally, the maximum file size depends on your device's memory. Desktop browsers handle files up to 2GB smoothly.

Will this service remain free forever?

Yes. videotomp3.tools is 100% free forever. Local processing keeps our operating costs near zero, allowing us to keep it completely free for everyone.
